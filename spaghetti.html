Beef free spaghetti

Description

This will be a recipe of vegan-spaghetti,

it won't be vegan but it will be MEATFREE

Sauce Ingridients

  • 1 large yellow onions
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tbs of Olive oil
  • 1 x Crushed Tomatoes: 500g
  • 400g minced meatsubstitute
  • 1 tbs of AllSpice
  • 1 tsp of cumin
  • 3/4 tsp of oregano
  • 1 1/2 tsp of paprika

Steps

  1. Chopp the onions and garlic.
  2. Fry the onions in the olive oil until it turns golden before putting in the garlic.
  3. Add the meatsubstitute together with the spices.
  4. Finally add the crushed tomato and let the newly formed sauce simmer for ten minutes.
  5. fill a pot with water and put in salt
  6. wait until the water starts boiling before putting in the pasta
  7. once the pasta is cooked, put int the pasta in the sauce let it soak in the flavor before digging in
