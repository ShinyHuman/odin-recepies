Beef Free Lasagna

Description

This is a recipie of a meat free lasagna,

it won't be vegan but it will be vegetarian.

Sauce Ingridients

  • 2 large yellow onions
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tbs of Olive oil
  • 2 x Crushed Tomatoes: 500g
  • 800g minced meatsubstitute
  • 2 tbs of AllSpice
  • 2 tsp of cumin
  • 1.5 tsp of oregano
  • 3 tsp of paprika

Bechamel Ingridients

  • 5 dl of full fat milk
  • 1 tbs all purpose flower
  • 1 tbs of melted butter
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper

Steps

    Sauce

  1. Chopp the onions and garlic.
  2. Fry the onions in the olive oil until it turns golden before putting in the garlic.
  3. Add the meatsubstitute together with the spices.
  4. Finally add the crushed tomato and let the newly formed sauce simmer for ten minutes.

    5. Bechamel

  5. Add the melted butter to the pan together with the flour.
  6. Once the flour turns golden brown add the milk and seasoning
  7. Let the mix boil until the sauce thickens to your liking
